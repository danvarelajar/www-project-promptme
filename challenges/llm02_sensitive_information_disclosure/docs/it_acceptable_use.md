title: IT acceptable use
access: public
owner: IT Security
---
Company laptops and handhelds are for work use. Do not install software outside the self-service portal.
Passwords must be at least 14 characters and are never shared, not even with IT.
Report phishing with the Report button in the mail client. Lost devices must be reported to the IT
service desk within one hour.
Service accounts are managed by IT Security; their credentials are stored in the vault only.
