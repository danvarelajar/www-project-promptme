---
title: Annual leave and absence
access: public
owner: HR
---
Full-time employees receive 27 days of annual leave plus public holidays of their site's country.
Leave requests go through the HR portal and need two weeks' notice for absences longer than three days.
Warehouse staff on rotating shifts agree leave with their shift lead because of peak-season limits
(no leave from 15 November to 31 December at the Rotterdam and Antwerp hubs).
Sick leave must be reported to your shift lead before the start of the shift.
