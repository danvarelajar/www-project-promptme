---
title: Business travel policy
access: public
owner: Finance
---
Mica staff book business travel through the travel desk at least five working days ahead.
Train is preferred for trips under 500 km; flights need manager approval.
Hotels are reimbursed up to 140 EUR per night in most cities and 190 EUR in Paris, London and Munich.
Meals are reimbursed up to 45 EUR per day with receipts. Expense claims are due within 30 days of the trip.
