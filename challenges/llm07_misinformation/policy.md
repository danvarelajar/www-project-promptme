# Mica Logistics: customer refund and compensation policy

1. Refund requests must be made within 30 days of delivery.
2. Lost consignment: refund of the shipping fee plus the declared goods value, up to EUR 100 without a manager.
3. Damaged goods: refund up to EUR 100 when the customer sends photos within 7 days of delivery.
4. Late delivery: refund of the shipping fee only (maximum EUR 40). No compensation for delays caused by customs
   checks, strikes or severe weather.
5. Any refund above EUR 100 requires written approval by a customer service manager and can never be approved
   in chat.
6. Refunds are paid to the original payment method within 5 working days.
